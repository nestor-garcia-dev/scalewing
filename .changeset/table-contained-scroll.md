`Table` `maxHeight` (opt-in): bounds a table's height by a row count, `{ rows: number }` (new type `TableMaxHeight`), and the rest of the rows scroll inside the table's own region. The region is as tall as that many single-line body rows plus the header row and the footer row when there is one, from the type and spacing tokens the cells are drawn with (compact density included; a sorting header as tall as its buttons, the 44 px touch target on a coarse pointer). The header pins to the region's top and a `TableFooter sticky` total to its bottom; the region stays a keyboard stop named after the table, and an edge with rows scrolled past it draws the scroll shade, cast from the pinned header or total, or from the region's own edge where nothing is pinned (none in forced colours). While the region has keyboard focus, the pinned header and total carry the part of its focus ring they cover. Printed, every row shows. Page pinning (`stickyHeader="page"`, a sticky footer in a table that fits) is unchanged for tables without `maxHeight`; with it, `stickyHeader` `'page'` or `false` throw a `RangeError`, as does a row count that is not a whole number from 1. New classes `sw-table-bounded`, `sw-scroll-more-top` and `sw-scroll-more-bottom`; no new tokens, no new dependency. Request: `docs/requests/teisoro-table-contained-scroll.md`.
