`Field` `changed` now also tints the control's fill (`docs/requests/teisoro-field-validation.md`, 2026-10-09 follow-up, Teisoro F-006-S11 task 1890), so a changed field no longer reads as the focused one: beside the accent border it keeps, the native control or the adorned frame takes the accent at 12 % as a background image over its own fill, which focus never draws. The value keeps 4.5:1 or more on the tint in every palette and scheme (6.74:1 at the lowest); the tint is on the text controls and select only (not a range, color or file input); while a placeholder shows the fill stays plain; on the tint an adorned field's prefix and suffix take the text color; a select keeps its chevron above it, placed for its size (`xs` included) by a private `--sw-select-chevron-inset` the select rule sets; and forced colors drop it and keep the `Highlight` border. No API change: same prop, same `sw-field-changed` class, no new tokens or dependencies. A consumer that layered its own background image on a changed control (none should: Scalewing owns the canvas) would now see it replaced.

`BarChartItem` takes an optional `tone` (`docs/requests/teisoro-bar-chart.md`, 2026-10-09 follow-up, Teisoro F-006-S11 task 1890): `'accent'`, `'success'`, `'warning'` or `'danger'` (new exported type `BarChartTone` and list `barChartTones`) colors a bar by what it means in place of its sign's default, such as a diverging chart's overage in warning and shortage in danger. New generated classes `sw-bar-chart-fill-accent`, `sw-bar-chart-fill-success`, `sw-bar-chart-fill-warning` and `sw-bar-chart-fill-danger`; a toned negative bar keeps `sw-bar-chart-fill-negative` and its diverging shape. Without a tone a chart is unchanged. An unknown tone throws a `RangeError`. Forced colors now draw every bar in `CanvasText`: a token fill was painted as the canvas there, so bars were invisible on their tracks. No new tokens and no new dependencies.
