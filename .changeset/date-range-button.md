`DateRangeButton`: a button showing a range of days (`value` `{ start, end }`, `YYYY-MM-DD`) that opens a calendar to pick a first and a last day. From `md` up it is a popover with the `presets` (`DateRangePreset`: `id`, `label`, `range`; a press applies it at once) beside two months (above them below `lg`), the range being picked in a polite status, and Cancel and Apply; below `md` it is a Dialog bottom sheet with the presets in a row and one month. The first press sets the start and the second the end; days after `max` are disabled, and once a start is pressed so are the days past `maxDays`. Apply calls `onChange` with a changed range; Cancel, Escape, a press outside and the sheet's close keep the value. Its name is `label`, `labels.nameSeparator` and the range it shows. `labels` (`DateRangeButtonLabels`) holds every word, `dayCount` included; `min`, `today`, `weekStartsOn`, `locale`, `disabled`, `id`, `size` and `variant` (default `neutral`) as on CalendarButton. Input it cannot draw throws a `RangeError`. New exports `DateRangeButton`, `DateRangeButtonProps`, `DateRangeButtonValue`, `DateRangePreset`, `DateRangeButtonLabels`; new `sw-date-range-*` classes. It shares CalendarButton's private calendar grid, popover and month steps, whose public behaviour is unchanged. `ButtonGroup attached` now finds its buttons by type, so an open calendar beside one keeps the group's dividers and corners. No new tokens, no new dependency. Request: `docs/requests/teisoro-date-range-button.md`.
