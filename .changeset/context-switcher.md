`ContextSwitcher`: a button showing the context in use (a workspace, a competition, a season) that opens a non-modal popup on the top layer to switch it: an optional filter field (an ARIA combobox that narrows the options by label or detail, case and accents ignored, its empty words shown and announced), the options in groups as a listbox (each with a mark, a detail, a status `Badge` and the shared count pill, all read in its name; the current one checked and `aria-selected`), and footer commands. Arrow keys, Enter, Home/End and typeahead in the listbox; Escape closes it with focus back on the trigger; a press outside or focus leaving closes it. `triggerAppearance` `framed` (default), `text`, `pill` or `icon` (a glyph alone, named by the label and the current option); `width="full"` fills a sidebar's top; `align` start or end. 44 px targets on a coarse pointer, right-to-left and forced colours covered. New classes `sw-context-switcher*` (its count joins the shared pill as `sw-context-switcher-count`); invalid groups, options, words or props throw a `RangeError`. No new tokens or dependencies. Request: `docs/requests/futmas-context-switcher.md`.
