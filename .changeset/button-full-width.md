`Button` `width`: `'content'` (the default, as before) or `'full'`, which fills the container's inline size in a `Stack`, a `Grid` cell or a column, and in an `Inline` row takes the space its siblings leave (as a full `Select` does; in a wrapping row it grows from its words and wraps), such as an Edit beside a ••• `ActionMenu` that keeps its own square at the row's end. The words stay centred. An unknown width throws a `RangeError`. New class `sw-button-full`; `ButtonWidth` and `buttonWidths` are exported. No new tokens or dependencies. Request: `docs/requests/teisoro-button-full-width.md`.
