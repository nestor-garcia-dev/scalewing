`LineChart`: a value over time, `points` (`LineChartPoint`: a `YYYY-MM-DD` `x` and a value `y`, in date order) placed by the days between them, `curve` `'linear'` or `'step'` (each value held until the next date), an optional `area` tinted from the line's stroke `tone` (default `accent`), `markers` (`LineChartMarker`: a downward triangle and words at one of the points' dates, in a stroke tone; words that would meet are left out of the drawing) with `markersLabel` heading their table column, `ticks` (the dates the time axis labels, thinned from the last), a boxed `highlight` period (`LineChartPeriod`), the last value beside the plot (`endLabel`), and `formatValue`, `formatTick` and `formatDate`. It is laid out for its measured width like ColumnChart, never scrolling sideways; the drawing is `aria-hidden` and a visually hidden table captioned by `label` (its value column `valueLabel`) carries every date, value and marker, the boxed rows `aria-current`. Input it cannot draw throws a `RangeError`. New exports `LineChart`, `LineChartProps`, `LineChartPoint`, `LineChartMarker`, `LineChartPeriod`, `LineChartCurve`, `lineChartCurves`; new classes `sw-chart-area`, `sw-chart-marker`, `sw-chart-marker-label`; no new tokens, no new dependency.
