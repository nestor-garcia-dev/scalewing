Muted words on a row's hover fill now keep 4.5:1: while a pressable `FixtureRow`, `ListRow` or `TableRow` shows the quiet fill (hovered, a ListRow or TableRow pressed, a TableRow's action focused), the parts on it read `--sw-color-muted` as the text colour, including the meta, details, a muted `TableCell`, the chevron and a consumer's `Text color="muted"` (muted was 4.14:1 on the fill in `ink` light, 3.47:1 in `harvest` dark). No prop, nothing changes at rest, toned words keep their tone, forced colours unchanged. `LiveRegion` (new, `LiveRegionProps`, `LiveRegionPoliteness`, `liveRegionPolitenesses`): a status line whose changing words are announced, an always-mounted region that starts empty (`role="status"` polite by default, or `role="alert"` with `politeness="assertive"`, `aria-atomic`), its string `children` swapped in as a new node on each change or `announceKey` change (the first words once it has been mounted 100 ms), drawn inline in `Text`'s look (`variant`, `color`) or `visuallyHidden`; an unknown politeness throws a `RangeError`, a look with `visuallyHidden` a `TypeError`. `AppShell` `skipLink` (opt-in, `AppShellSkipLink`: `{ label }`): a skip link first in the shell, hidden until focused, then fixed at the window's top start; a press moves focus to `main`, which gets an id and `tabindex="-1"` only with it, without changing the address; a blank label throws a `RangeError`. `Select` `disabled` (opt-in): a native disabled trigger, out of the Tab order and faded (`GrayText` in forced colours), its list never opens and `onChange` never runs. New class `sw-app-shell-skip-link` and stacking layer `skipLink` (7). SiteHeader's skip link now shares its look's rules (`css/skip-link.ts`); its output is unchanged apart from rule order.
