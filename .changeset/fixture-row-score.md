`FixtureRow` `score` (opt-in, `FixtureScore`: `{ label, home, away, onChange(side, value), onSubmit(), onCancel?(), disabled?, error? }`, boxes `FixtureScoreBox`: `{ value, label, ref?, invalid? }`): two score boxes between the sides in place of `center` (now optional when `score` is given), digits only (`inputmode="numeric"`, two digits, selected on focus) in a group named by `label`, each box named by its own `label`; Enter calls `onSubmit`, Escape `onCancel`; 32 px squares on a fine pointer and 44 px on a coarse one; `error` is said politely under the row and marks the boxes invalid. A score row is never a link or a button, and works with `flag`, `select` and `action`. `FixtureRow` `detail` (opt-in): a full-width part under the row, outside its press area. `TableRow` `rule="after"` (opt-in, `TableRowRule`, `tableRowRules`): a 2 px muted rule under the row, such as a cut under the last playoff place, `CanvasText` in forced colors. New types `FixtureScore`, `FixtureScoreBox`, `FixtureScoreSide`, `TableRowRule`; new classes `sw-fixture-score`, `sw-fixture-score-box`, `sw-fixture-score-dash`, `sw-fixture-score-error`, `sw-fixture-item-below`, `sw-fixture-detail`, `sw-table-row-rule-after`.
