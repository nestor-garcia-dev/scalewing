`SectionNav` (`docs/requests/teisoro-section-nav.md`, Teisoro F-006-S11, ADM-1), a new component: the navigation between the sections of one area, such as a portal's pages. A labelled `nav` (`aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`) of links from `items` (`{ id, label, href, current?, icon? }`), quieter than a workspace's navigation: label-size words in the muted color, no fill, the current item in the text color over an accent underline on the row's hairline. `current` is `'page'`, `'location'` (a page inside the section) or a boolean, written as `aria-current`. `onNavigate?: (item) => void` takes a plain press (main button, no modifier key) after preventing the browser's navigation, for a client router; a modified or middle press keeps the browser's behavior. `verticalFrom?: Breakpoint` stacks the items as a side list from that breakpoint, the current one marked by a bar at its start. A coarse pointer gets 44 px targets; forced colors draw `LinkText` and a `Highlight` mark. Empty or duplicate ids and empty labels throw a `RangeError`. New exported types `SectionNavItem`, `SectionNavProps`; new generated classes `sw-section-nav`, `sw-section-nav-list`, `sw-section-nav-link`, `sw-section-nav-icon`, `sw-section-nav-vertical-from-md`. No new tokens and no new dependencies.
