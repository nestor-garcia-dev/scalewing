`Dialog` `sheetBelow` and `closeLabel` (`docs/requests/fantasy-football-dialog-sheet.md`, the fantasy-football companion's phone review dialogs). `sheetBelow?: Breakpoint` (`'md'`): below that breakpoint the dialog is a bottom sheet, docked to the bottom edge at the full viewport width, top corners at `--sw-radius-lg` and bottom corners square, a `space-8` strip of backdrop left above it (the same dvh-aware cap), and bottom padding that clears `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`; it slides up on open with `--sw-motion-default`, not under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and draws no grabber. From the breakpoint up it is the centered dialog at its `size`. `closeLabel?: string`: an icon-only ghost close `Button` ends the title row, named by the label, and calls `onClose`; the title still names the dialog and `titleLevel` still applies. The button is the first control, so it takes the focus on open unless content has `autoFocus`. Its X is private control chrome drawn in CSS (no icon dependency). Without `closeLabel` the markup is unchanged. New generated classes `sw-dialog-sheet-below-md`, `sw-dialog-header`, `sw-dialog-close`, `sw-dialog-close-glyph`. No new tokens and no new dependencies.
