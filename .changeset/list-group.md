Web `ListGroup` and `ListRow`, the counterpart of react-native's. `ListGroup` is a list (`ul`, role kept) of rows with hairlines between them, a bordered `panel` or `plain` rows inside a surface, named by `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`; `maxHeight` (`{ rows }`) bounds it to rows of a title and one detail line that scroll inside a named, focusable region shaded at an edge with more past it, and `footer` stays pinned under the rows, such as a total. `ListRow` has a `title` (`titleAppearance` `strong` or `plain`, `tone` `danger`), a muted `detail`, a `value` with `valueDetail` and `valueTone` (a table cell's tones) and a hidden `leading` mark; `onPress` or `href` makes the whole row a button or link with a chevron, named by its title and value and described by its details (`href` with `onPress` takes a plain press for a client router); `selected` makes it a choice with a check (`aria-pressed` or `aria-current`); `accessory="none"` hides the chevron; `disabled` blocks presses. Input it cannot draw throws a `RangeError`. New exports `ListGroup`, `ListGroupProps`, `ListRow`, `ListRowProps`, `listGroupVariants`, `listRowTones`, `listRowTitleAppearances`, `listRowValueTones`, `ListGroupMaxHeight`, `ListGroupVariant`, `ListRowTone`, `ListRowTitleAppearance`, `ListRowValueTone`; new `sw-list*` classes; no new tokens, no new dependency.
