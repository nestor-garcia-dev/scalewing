`SegmentedControl` item `icon` (`docs/requests/teisoro-segmented-control-icon.md`, Teisoro F-011 release R3): a consumer mark (such as a `GlyphTile`) before an item's label, `aria-hidden`. A track with icons takes the `md` control height and label type, a subtle track fill and a raised selected item; a track without icons is unchanged. New generated classes `sw-segmented-with-icons` and `sw-segmented-item-icon`. No new tokens or dependencies.
