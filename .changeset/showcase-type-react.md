`Text` takes the tokens' new variants `hero` (an `h1` by default), `headline` (an `h2`) and `lead` (a `p`), each stepping down to its compact size below `md` like `display` and `heading`. New classes `sw-text-hero`, `sw-text-headline`, `sw-text-lead`, generated from the tokens. Needs `@scalewing/tokens` 1.7.0. Request: `docs/requests/teisoro-showcase-type.md`.
