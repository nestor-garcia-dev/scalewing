`DenominationGrid` `labelWidth` (`docs/requests/teisoro-denomination-grid.md`, 2026-10-08 follow-up, Teisoro F-006-S11, DRW-27 and VLT-14). `labelWidth?: 'xs' | 'sm' | 'md' | 'lg' | 'xl'` (4, 6, 8, 12 and 16 rem, the `Table` column sizes) lines a strip's columns up with every other strip of the same columns in a container of the same width, such as the cards of a feed: the row labels take that width and the count columns (and the total column from md up) share the rest of the strip's scroll region equally, measured from the region (a size container, `100cqi`) rather than from the counts, so every column is sized and no content moves one. A count wider than its share widens its column and the strip scrolls as a plain one does; below md the total column still takes no width. Without it each strip sizes its columns by its own content, as before. The tiles layout ignores it. An unknown width throws a `RangeError`. New exported type `DenominationLabelWidth`; new generated classes `sw-denomination-scroll-aligned`, `sw-denomination-strip-aligned`, `sw-denomination-label-{xs,sm,md,lg,xl}`; the strip sets `--sw-denomination-columns` and `--sw-denomination-total` on its table. No new tokens and no new dependencies.
