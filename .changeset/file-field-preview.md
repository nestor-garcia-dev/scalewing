`FileField` `value.preview`: the address of a small image of a chosen photo, such as an object URL the consumer made from the `File`, drawn as a decorative 3:2 thumbnail (a space-8 by a space-6, cropped to fill, at the small radius) before the file's name in place of `icon`. A blank preview throws a `RangeError`. New class `sw-file-field-preview`. No new tokens, no new dependency. Request: `docs/requests/teisoro-file-field-preview.md`.
