Additive. A generic categorical colour scale (`docs/requests/categorical-colors.md`, Teisoro F-011 release R3): `categoricalHues` (`green`, `blue`, `orange`, `purple`, `teal`, `rose`), `CategoricalHue`, `CategoricalColor` (a hue or `neutral`), `categoricalColorKeys`, `categoricalColors` (`{ light, dark }`, the same fills in both schemes), `categoricalColorFor(color, scheme)` (throws a `RangeError` on an unknown colour), `isCategoricalColor`, `onCategorical` (`#FFFFFF`, the glyph drawn on a fill), `categoricalGlyphContrast` and `categoricalGlyphMinContrast` (3, WCAG 1.4.11 for a glyph). Every fill keeps a white glyph at 3:1 or more (a glyph, WCAG 1.4.11; not a text background) and stands 3:1 apart from the default light and dark surface (palettes with a strong surface may sit closer); orange is darkened from `#E08600` (2.77:1) in 1% steps toward black to `#D57F00` (3.06:1), the others are unchanged. `categoricalColors` is frozen. Hues tell a small set of kinds apart; they carry no meaning and palettes do not overlay them. The semantic colours, `Theme` and `createTheme` are unchanged. Renderers pick it up through their caret dependency (ADR 0009): `@scalewing/react` generates `--sw-categorical-<colour>` for `GlyphTile` in its next minor; native may import it directly.

Renderer sign-off: the owner delegated plan approval to Claude for Teisoro F-011 (Teisoro decisions O-1 to O-3 and D-1), and Claude, acting for the owner, signed off for both the `@scalewing/react` and the `@scalewing/react-native` agents. The change is additive, so neither renderer needs a change to keep working.
