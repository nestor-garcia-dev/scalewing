New class `sw-link-external-mark`. `Stepper` `onCommit` and `commitDelay` (opt-in): the finished count once per entry, on blur, on Enter and `commitDelay` ms (default 600) after the last − / + press or step key, never per typed digit and never for an unchanged count; `onChange` is unchanged.
