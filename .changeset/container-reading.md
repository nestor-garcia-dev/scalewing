`sw-container-reading`: a utility class that centres a column at the reading width (`--sw-reading-max`, 46rem, `Article`'s), as `sw-container` does at the container width, and full width below it; for a form or text that reads better narrower than the page. No new tokens, no new dependency. Request: `docs/requests/teisoro-container-reading.md`.
