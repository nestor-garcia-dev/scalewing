Native `Calendar` shows the `DateField` month grid inline and always open,
with the same value, bounds, locale, and navigation labels; it opens on the
selected month and stays open after a pick. `MultiSelect` takes
`variant="list"` for full-width checkbox rows with a check mark instead of
chips (see `docs/requests/futmas-schedule-steps.md`).
