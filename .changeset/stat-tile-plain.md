`StatTile` `appearance="plain"` (`StatTileAppearance`, `statTileAppearances`; default `card`): the figure without the tile's surface, edge, radius or padding, top-aligned, for figures inside a card, such as four answers in a row divided by a composed `Separator`. A plain tile cannot be `emphasis="primary"`; that or an unknown appearance throws a `RangeError`. New class `sw-stat-tile-plain`; no new tokens, no new dependency. Request: `docs/requests/teisoro-stat-tile-plain.md`.
