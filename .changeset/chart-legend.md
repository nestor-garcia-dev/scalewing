`ChartLegend`: a chart's key, a list (`role="list"` kept) of items, each an `aria-hidden` swatch and its words, in a wrapping row in the caption type. `items` (`ChartLegendItem[]`) take a `label`, a `swatch` (`ChartLegendSwatch`: `bar`, `line`, `marker` as a downward triangle, `box` as the boxed current period's dashed outline) and a `tone`: a `bar` a fill tone (`ChartFillTone`: the categorical colours, and `DataBar`'s statement tones), the others a stroke tone (`ChartStrokeTone`: `accent`, `success`, `warning`, `danger`, `neutral`); anything else, or an empty label, throws a `RangeError`. Every chart mark reads one tone table (`sw-chart-tone-<tone>` classes handing `--sw-chart-fill`, `--sw-chart-stroke` and `--sw-chart-ink` to the marks inside), so a swatch is the colour of the mark it names; fills keep 1.5:1, strokes 3:1 and inks 4.5:1 on the surface in every palette and scheme. `DataBar`'s fills now come from that table, unchanged. Forced colours draw every swatch in the system text colour. New exports `ChartLegend`, `ChartLegendProps`, `ChartLegendItem`, `ChartLegendSwatch`, `chartLegendSwatches`, `ChartTone`, `ChartFillTone`, `chartFillTones`, `ChartStrokeTone`, `chartStrokeTones`; new classes `sw-chart-legend`, `sw-chart-legend-item`, `sw-chart-legend-swatch`, `sw-chart-legend-<swatch>` and `sw-chart-tone-<tone>`; no new tokens, no new dependency.
