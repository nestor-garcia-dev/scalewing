Scalewing request from Teisoro.

Status: released in `@scalewing/react` 1.30.0 (PR #133; Teisoro F-011-S05, release R4 "ledger and navigation", item 15; the redesign inventory's row 15 and risk 7).
Renderer: react
Missing surface: `TableFooter`, a table footer for a total row that can stay pinned to the bottom edge while the rows scroll.
Why Box/Stack/Inline/Card/Text/Button/Field cannot do this: the cash ledger (ServicesDayLedger2) pins a row to the bottom of the table while the day's entries scroll under it (the opening count, or the filtered subtotal when Show hides kinds), and the inventory asks for a bold total row under a heavier rule. `Table` has `TableHeader` and `TableBody` but no footer, nothing pins a row to the bottom, and the stacking order has no layer between the sticky header cells and the sticky `ActionBar` (inventory risk 7). Teisoro owns no CSS.
Existing surface this might already be: `Table` `stickyHeader` (pins the header to the top of the table's own scroll region); `ActionBar` `summary` (a bar for the page's action, not a table row).
Workaround I almost used: the total as the table's last body row, unpinned.
Teisoro use: `CashLedger` (the Services day ledger, PosDay Change today, VaultToday).
Proposed API (inventory): `TableFooter` with sticky pinning and a bold total row under a heavier rule; a layer in `css/stacking.ts` ordered table cells, then the table footer, then the action bar.

## Accepted API (2026-10-11)

- `TableFooter` (props `TableFooterProps`: the `tfoot`'s attributes and `sticky?: boolean`, default `false`), a forwarded ref. Its cells are at weight 600 under a 2 px rule in the border colour across its first row (each cell's `::before`, 1 px over the last body row's border so it reads 2 px, so forced colours keep it as `CanvasText` and a sticky cell carries it). A `TableCell as="th" scope="row"` in it names the total and reads as body text (the row header reset from `teisoro-table-ledger.md`, #132).
- `sticky`: the footer's cells pin to the bottom edge (`bottom: 0`) on the sticky header's glass (solid surface under Reduce Transparency), on a new stacking layer `stickyFooter` (2), over the cells and the pinned header cells (`stickyCell`, 1) and under the sticky `ActionBar` (`bottomChrome`, now 3; `popupHost` 4 and `topChrome` 5 move up one, `popup` stays 10). The bar covers a pinned footer (both pin to the bottom edge), and so does a `Dialog`'s pinned footer: a page does not pair them. In forced colours the pinned footer and header cells are the solid system canvas.
- Where it pins: `Table` scrolls inside its own region, which would pin the footer to the region's bottom (never reached on a page that scrolls). A table's region now carries `sw-scroll-fits` once measured with its content fitting its width; a table region that holds a sticky footer and fits stops being a scroll box (`overflow: visible`), so the footer pins to the page's bottom edge and the sticky header to the page's top while the page scrolls. A table wider than its region (a phone with many columns) scrolls sideways in it and pins inside the region, as before. The pinned header slides under a sticky `AppHeader` (or any sticky top chrome), which covers it.
- Generated classes `sw-table-footer-sticky`, `sw-scroll-fits`.
- Sizes and looks on Scalewing's scale (Teisoro D-2): the rule is 2 px in the border colour (the board's pinned total uses a 2 px rule at 0.18 alpha, its body rows 0.10); the pinned row's fill is the header's glass (the board's white); the footer row keeps the table's density padding (the board's 48 rows). The board pins its opening count at the bottom without a heavier rule and its "Cash drawer" total at the top, under the header; a top-pinned total row is not built (put it in `TableHeader` if needed, where `stickyHeader` pins it).

Evidence: `table-footer.test.tsx` (the `tfoot`, the sticky class, weight 600, the 2 px rule and its forced-colours colour, the sticky rule on the `stickyFooter` layer, the fitting region's `overflow: visible`, reduced transparency); `stacking.test.ts` (sticky cell under the sticky footer under the ActionBar); `scroll-region.test.tsx` (the `sw-scroll-fits` mark: none unmeasured or on a DenominationGrid strip, dropped while overflowing and back when it fits); gallery "Table" ("Census by site", 30 rows and a sticky total); `apps/gallery/e2e/table-footer.spec.ts` on desktop-en, mobile-es and forced-colors (weight 600 and the 2 px rule, the region not a scroll box while it fits, the total pinned to the viewport's bottom over the rows, the header pinned to the top, forced colours solid, the rule right to left; a table widened past its region scrolls and pins inside it, and fits again when narrowed). The gallery's own sticky header covers the pinned table header there, as an AppHeader would.
