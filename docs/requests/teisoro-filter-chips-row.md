Scalewing request from Teisoro.

Status: released in `@scalewing/react` 1.43.0 (PR #209; accepted 2026-10-20; Teisoro F-014-S07 "Scalewing fidelity", task 2630; the gap Teisoro F-014-S04 task 2615 found on the training boards Main, TrainingSection, PhoneTraining and PhoneSection; plan approval under the owner's F-014 delegation of 2026-10-19).
Renderer: react
Missing surface: two web `FilterChips` groups that wrap or scroll sideways together on one row, one set apart at the row's end.
Why Box/Stack/Inline/Card/Text/Button/Field cannot do this: the Library's chips are the kinds (one choice) and, for managers, "Missing a translation" at the row's end, a toggle of another kind (a checkbox that narrows any kind). On a phone the boards scroll them sideways as one strip. In Teisoro they are two groups in an `Inline`: the kinds scroll in their own box and Missing a translation drops to its own line, at the next line's start rather than its end. `Inline` cannot scroll, and a scrolling `FilterChips` scrolls only its own chips.
Existing surface this might already be: `FilterChips` `overflow="scroll"` (one group's chips); `Inline` `wrap` and `justify="between"` (no scroll; a wrapped end group lands at its line's start).
Options weighed: (1) a slot on `FilterChips` for a trailing chip of another group: the second group would sit inside the first's `fieldset` and be read as part of it ("Kinds, Missing a translation, group"), and a group would own another's layout; (2) one `FilterChips` holding a chip of another kind: mixes a radio and a checkbox in one named group; (3) an `Inline` that scrolls: a scrolling group inside it still scrolls on its own, so the `Inline` would need rules about `FilterChips`. Chosen: (4) a small `FilterChipsRow` beside `FilterChips`, a plain `div` around sibling groups. It is the smallest surface that keeps each group's semantics whole, it has two props besides its children, and its rules live with the chips' own CSS, which they override.
Teisoro use: `training/TrainingFilters`'s `TrainingChips` (the Library and a section page), desktop and phone.
Proposed API: `FilterChipsRow` with `children`, `end` and `overflow`.

## Accepted API (2026-10-20)

- `FilterChipsRow` (`FilterChipsRowProps`): `children` (the groups), `end?: ReactNode` (a group set apart at the row's end) and `overflow?: 'wrap' | 'scroll'` (`filterChipsOverflows`), default `wrap`.
- A plain `div` (`sw-filter-chips-row`) with no role: each group keeps its own name, choices and Tab stops. The groups sit a space-2 apart, their chips level along the bottom (a group may draw its label above; an error under one group lifts its chips above the others').
- `end` (`sw-filter-chips-row-end`) has `margin-inline-start: auto`: at the row's end while the groups fit, at its own line's end when the row wraps. An empty `end` (`undefined`, `null`, a boolean, `''`) draws nothing; the shared `hasSlot` check moved from the list rows to `components/slot.ts`.
- `wrap`: a group is as wide as its chips and wraps (or scrolls, with its own `overflow="scroll"`) inside the row when it is wider than the row.
- `scroll` (`sw-filter-chips-row-scroll`): every group's chips stay on one line and the row is the one box that scrolls sideways, never the page, padded by the focus ring's reach and pulled back as a scrolling group is, with a thin scrollbar and contained overscroll; a group's own scroll gives way (no wrap, no scroll, no margin or padding), so its chips scroll with the rest, and a group is as wide as its chips (`width: min-content`), so a drawn label or an error wraps under them rather than widening it; a focused chip is scrolled into view by the browser. The row is positioned, so a group's visually hidden legend, placed where it would sit past the edge, stays inside the box rather than widening the page.
- An unknown overflow throws a `RangeError`. No new tokens, layers or dependencies. New classes `sw-filter-chips-row`, `sw-filter-chips-row-end`, `sw-filter-chips-row-scroll` (`css/css-filter-chips-row.ts`, after the chips' layout rules).

Evidence: `filter-chips-row.test.tsx` (the groups and the end box in order with their own names, no role, the scroll class, no end box for an empty end, the refusal, the generated rules after the groups' scroll rules, a scrolling group at its chips' width, the positioned scroll box, the catalog); gallery "FilterChipsRow" (guides by kind with a warning "Not translated" toggle at the end, scrolling and wrapping, and a scrolling row with drawn labels and an error); `apps/gallery/e2e/filter-chips-row.spec.ts` on desktop-en, mobile-es and forced-colors (every chip of both groups on one line, only the row scrolling, the end group at the row's end at 1280, on a phone the end chip past the edge reached by Tab and scrolled into view, the toggle working, the wrapping row's end group at its own line's end, the kinds inside the row, a scrolling group with a drawn label and an error as wide as its chips with the error wrapping, no sideways page scroll).
