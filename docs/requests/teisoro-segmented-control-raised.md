Scalewing request from Teisoro.

Status: released in `@scalewing/react` 1.32.0 (PR #146; accepted 2026-10-11; Teisoro F-011 release R6 "canvas fidelity"; plan approval under Teisoro decisions O-1 to O-4, D-1, D-2; closes the segmented-control item of decision D-37).
Renderer: react
Missing surface: `SegmentedControl` `variant="raised"`. An extension of `SegmentedControl` (`teisoro-segmented-control.md`, `teisoro-segmented-control-icon.md`), not a new component.
Why Box/Stack/Inline/Card/Text/Button/Field cannot do this: on about twenty F-011 boards (the entry steps' service switcher with its tiles, EntryStep1/2, EntryMulti, NsfPay, StepCheck; the "Largest first / Twenties only" choice; DrawerAdjust; AuditReview2) a one-of choice is a grey track (#e8e8ed, radius 10 to 12) whose selected segment is white with a small shadow, the other labels in the body color. Scalewing's compact track is a glass pill with muted unselected labels (an icon track moves it to the subtle fill but keeps the muted labels and the pill), and the filled track paints the selection in the accent. Teisoro owns no CSS. D-37 listed this as a look not matched in R3.
Existing surface this might already be: `variant="compact"` with item icons (the subtle track and raised selection, but muted labels, the pill shape, and only with icons).
Workaround I almost used: none that keeps one radiogroup.
Teisoro use: the service switcher and every two- or three-way choice in a step.
Proposed API: `variant="raised"` (generic: the selected segment is raised off a grey track).

## Accepted API (2026-10-11)

- `SegmentedControlVariant` gains `'raised'`; `compact` stays the default and `filled` is unchanged.
- `raised`: the track is `quietFill` (`color-mix(in srgb, var(--sw-color-muted) 16%, transparent)`, about #E8E8E9 on white, the board's #E8E8ED) with a transparent border and `calc(var(--sw-radius-sm) + 2px)` corners (concentric with the segments' `sm` radius around the 2 px track inset), sized to its segments even in a Stack (`align-self: flex-start` in its field). Each segment: `sm` radius, the md control height, label type (15/20) at weight 500 in `--sw-color-text` (the board's #3A3A3C/#1D1D1F; 5.29:1 or more on the fill in every palette), `space-4` across (`space-2`/`space-3` with a mark). The selected segment: `--sw-color-surface`, `--sw-elevation-sm`, weight 600 (the boards use 600 and 700; 600 is the label weight). In the dark scheme a shadow does not show and the surface is about the track's grey, so the selected segment draws `inset 0 0 0 1px var(--sw-color-muted)` (3:1 or more against the track over the canvas or a card in every dark palette, tested); the light scheme keeps the board's white segment and shadow. The track's transparent border would win over `.sw-segmented[aria-invalid='true']` (same specificity, earlier), so the danger border and its forced-colours Mark are restated after the raised rules. Forced colors outline the track (CanvasText) and the selected segment (Highlight).
- With item icons the raised look holds; the compact icon rules now read `:not(.sw-segmented-filled, .sw-segmented-raised)`, one `:not()` so their specificity (and the error outline's precedence) is unchanged.
- Sizes (D-2): segments 44 (board 40), track inset 2 (board 3 to 4), radius 8 + 2 (board 8 to 9 and 10 to 12).
- Generated class `sw-segmented-raised`, rules in `css-segmented-raised.ts`.

Evidence: `segmented-raised.test.tsx` (default unchanged, the class, radio semantics, change and arrow keys, the dark ring's 3:1 on every dark palette, the restated error outline and its order, the class with marks, the generated rules and forced colours, the compact icon selector); `segmented-icon.test.tsx` (the compact icon rules with the new `:not()`); gallery "SegmentedControl" ("Survey, raised" with marks, "Count order" words only, "Closed count order" disabled, "Count order to retake" with an error); `apps/gallery/e2e/segmented-raised.spec.ts` on desktop-en, mobile-es and forced-colors (the grey track, the white raised selection, text-color words at 4.5:1 or more, sized to its segments, narrower than its Stack, 44 px segments, arrow keys, the marked track raised; the outline in forced colours; the danger border, Mark in forced colours, the disabled track; the dark ring) and `segmented-control.spec.ts` (the icon track's error outline still wins).
