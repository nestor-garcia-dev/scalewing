Scalewing request from Teisoro.

Status: accepted 2026-10-11 for the `@scalewing/react` 1.30.0 release (Teisoro F-011-S05, release R4 "ledger and navigation", item 14; the redesign inventory's row 15).
Renderer: react
Missing surface: ledger extensions to `Table`: a toned cell, rules between columns, a sub row under its entry and a struck (voided) row.
Why Box/Stack/Inline/Card/Text/Button/Field cannot do this: the cash ledger (ServicesDayLedger2, LedgerFix1/3, VoidOne, VoidBatchOne, VoidRows, PosDay, PosDaySaved, VaultToday) reads across as well as down. It needs vertical hairlines between the bill columns, green figures for cash in, a batch's money orders and a Get change's parts indented under their entry, and a voided row greyed and struck but kept on the record. `Text color` inside a cell colours a span, not the cell; nothing draws column rules, indents a row's header or strikes a row, and Teisoro owns no CSS.
Existing surface this might already be: `Table` / `TableRow` / `TableCell` (`stickyHeader`, `layout="fixed"`, `numeric`, `width`, `selected`); `DenominationGrid` `cellTones` (counts, not a ledger).
Workaround I almost used: `Text color="success"` in each cell and an inline `style` for the rules and the strike (not allowed).
Teisoro use: `CashLedger` (`services-day/ActivityCard.tsx` rebuilt): the Services day ledger, PosDay Change today, VaultToday and the Void boards.
Proposed API (inventory): `TableCell tone?: 'success' | 'danger' | 'muted' | 'accent'`, `Table columnRules?: boolean`, `TableRow variant?: 'sub' | 'struck'`.

## Accepted API (2026-10-11)

- `TableCell` `tone?: TableCellTone` (`'success' | 'danger' | 'muted' | 'accent'`; `tableCellTones` exported): the cell's text in the theme's semantic colour, as `Text color` uses it. A colour is never the only signal (keep the sign or the words; cash out stays the default text, never red).
- `Table` `columnRules?: boolean` (default `false`): a hairline at the inline start of every cell after a row's first, mirrored right to left; a sticky header cell draws it as a border on its own `::after` (a positioned cell's neighbour covers a collapsed border), so forced colours keep it (`CanvasText`) there too.
- `TableRow` `variant?: TableRowVariant` (`'default' | 'sub'`; `tableRowVariants` exported) and `struck?: boolean`. **Changed from the proposal:** `struck` is its own flag, not a third `variant`, because VoidRows strikes a sub row (Money order 3 of a batch); one `variant` could not say both.
  - `sub`: the row's header cell (`TableCell as="th" scope="row"`) is indented a `space-6` step (32) past the cell's padding, with a 2 px guide bar in the border colour at the indent (a border, so forced colours keep it as `CanvasText`). A sub row without a row header is not indented.
  - `struck`: the cells on the subtle fill, the text muted and struck through, toned cells muted too. A line-through is not read aloud, so the row must say why in words, such as a `Badge` "Voided". A Badge that sits in a line of text (an atomic inline box) is not struck; a Badge that is itself a flex or grid item (a direct child of `Inline` or `Stack`) is struck with the row, so put it in the title's line.
- A row header in the body or footer (`th scope="row"`) now reads as body text (inherits colour and font) instead of the column header's muted caption; the reset is no stronger than `.sw-table th`, so `numeric`, `tone` and `struck` still apply to a row header. No Scalewing or Teisoro page used a body `th scope="row"` in a `Table` before.
- Failure boundary: an unknown `tone` or `variant` throws a `RangeError`.
- Contrast: the tones are the theme tokens, 4.5:1 on the surface and the canvas in the default palettes and most named ones. Gaps under 4.5:1 today, a tokens matter (the same as `Text color`): sunburst and harvest light success on the canvas; sunburst, harvest and mocha dark danger on the surface (and harvest and mocha dark danger on the canvas); harvest dark accent on the surface. Muted on the struck row's subtle fill keeps 4.5:1 in every palette (tested; the closest is 4.54).
- Sizes and looks on Scalewing's scale (Teisoro D-2): rules are the border colour at one weight (the board draws the first bill column's rule heavier, 0.18 against 0.10); the sub indent is 32 plus a 12 gap after a 2 px bar (board 32, a 2×22 bar, a 10 gap; ours is 24 high, `space-5`); the struck fill is `subtle` (board #fbfbfd); the struck row strikes every cell's text, so the "who, when, why" line under a voided title (VoidRows) is struck too unless it is a Badge in the title's line (the board leaves that line plain), and the struck original amount above "$0.00" in one cell (VoidRows) is struck along with "$0.00"; row heights stay the table's density (the board's 56 and 44).
- Generated classes `sw-table-tone-success`, `sw-table-tone-danger`, `sw-table-tone-muted`, `sw-table-tone-accent`, `sw-table-column-rules`, `sw-table-row-sub`, `sw-table-row-struck`.

Evidence: `table-ledger.test.tsx` (classes, a plain table unchanged, failures, the tone contrast with its named gaps, muted on subtle, the generated rules including forced colours, numeric and tone winning on a row header, the struck-over-tone order); gallery "Table" (the "Colony ledger" and a compact copy: column rules, success, danger, accent and muted cells, sub rows, a struck sub row, a struck row with a Badge); `apps/gallery/e2e/table-ledger.spec.ts` on desktop-en, mobile-es and forced-colors (the success colour, a 1 px rule on every cell after the first, the sub indent of at least 32 and its 2 px bar, the row header at body weight, line-through on every struck cell, the struck row header and toned cell muted, a struck sub row indented, the accent colour, the sticky header's rules in forced colours and right to left, the compact sub indent).
