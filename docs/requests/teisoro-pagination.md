Scalewing request from Teisoro.

Status: accepted 2026-10-13 for `@scalewing/react` 1.38.0 (Teisoro F-012-S03, release M1 "tables that hold a period", item 4; the manager views inventory's row 16; plan approval under Teisoro decisions O-1, D-1 and D-6).
Renderer: react
Missing surface: `Pagination`, previous and next steps through a list shown a page at a time, beside the consumer's words for the range shown.
Why Box/Stack/Inline/Card/Text/Button/Field cannot do this: the owner's rule (Teisoro D-6) is that a list without a total shows 25 a page. The boards (ReportsCorrections, ReportsVault, ChecksRisk, AdminEmployees, ChecksBounced) draw "1–16 of 16 changes · 25 a page" in the muted caption with ‹ › steps at the end, greyed at either end. Teisoro could compose a `Button` pair, but the icon-only square, its chevron (private chrome, no icon catalog), the landmark, the live range and the end behaviour would be rebuilt in each of five reports.
Existing surface this might already be: `ButtonGroup attached` (two buttons, no landmark, no range, no end rule); `Steps` (a form's steps, not pages); `SectionNav` (links between sections).
Workaround I almost used: two `Button`s with "‹" and "›" text and `disabled` at the ends, which drops focus to the page when the last page is reached and reads "‹" aloud.
Teisoro use: `ReportTable` (F-012 composite) for every list without a total: Corrections' Every change and By person, the vault's trucks and audits, Risk management, Employees, the bounced checks. On a phone Teisoro uses a "Show 25 more" `Button` instead.
Proposed API (inventory): `page`, `pageCount`, `onPageChange`, `rangeLabel` (the consumer words "1–25 of 130 changes"), `labels`.

## Accepted API (2026-10-13)

- `Pagination` (props `PaginationProps`): a `nav`'s attributes but `children`, plus `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` (exactly one, required by the type, as `SectionNav`), `page: number` (from 1), `pageCount: number` (from 1), `onPageChange: (page: number) => void`, `rangeLabel: string` and `labels: PaginationLabels` (`{ previous: string; next: string }`, the steps' accessible names). A forwarded ref to the `nav`. No product copy: every word is the consumer's.
- Layout: a wrapping row, the range in the muted caption (tabular figures) at the inline start, the steps at the end (`margin-inline-start: auto`, so they stay at the end when the row wraps on a phone). The range is a polite, atomic live region, so a screen reader hears the new range after a step while focus stays on the step.
- Steps: `Button` `variant="neutral"` `size="sm"`, square (`iconButtonRules`, the CalendarButton and InfoTip rule), the 44 px touch target on a coarse pointer, each named by its label and holding the shared stroked chevron (`chevron.ts`, now `chevronStrokeIn(color)`, here in `currentColor`; borders, so forced colours keep it), pointing toward the inline start for previous and the end for next, one `:dir(rtl)` flip mirroring both. DateField's month steps keep their own SVG inside the calendar.
- **Ends: `aria-disabled`, never `disabled`.** At the first page the previous step, and at the last the next step, take `aria-disabled="true"` with `disabledAppearance="neutral"` (the board's grey), the 1.36.0 Button behaviour: it looks disabled, stays a Tab stop (every Button carries `tabindex="0"`, Safari included), and Pagination ignores its press. A natively disabled button would drop focus to the page the moment Next reached the last page. A single page greys both.
- Pages that are not whole numbers from 1 to `pageCount`, a `pageCount` under 1, or an empty step label (`labels.previous` or `labels.next`) throw a `RangeError`; the checks and the step arithmetic are pure (`pagination-pages.ts`: `assertPaginationPages`, `assertPaginationLabels`, `paginationStep`).
- The required-name union (`aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`) is now one private type, `src/accessible-name.ts` (`AccessibleName`), shared by `Pagination`, `SectionNav`, `Tabs` and `SegmentedControl`; their public props are unchanged.
- No new tokens, layers or dependencies. New classes `sw-pagination`, `sw-pagination-range`, `sw-pagination-steps`, `sw-pagination-step`, `sw-pagination-glyph`, `sw-pagination-glyph-previous` and `sw-pagination-glyph-next`.

### Differences from the boards (Teisoro D-2)

- The boards' steps are 32 px grey squares with a "‹" / "›" character; Scalewing's are the `sm` control (its token height) with a drawn chevron, and 44 px on a coarse pointer.
- The board writes "25 a page" in the same caption as the range; that stays the consumer's words inside `rangeLabel`.
