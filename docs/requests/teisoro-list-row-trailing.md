Scalewing request from Teisoro.

Status: released in `@scalewing/react` 1.42.0 (PR #199; accepted 2026-10-19; Teisoro F-014-S03 "Scalewing: file picker, PDF viewer, Markdown view, blue count", task 2610; the training library inventory's row 7 and §2; plan approval under the owner's F-014 delegation of 2026-10-19).
Renderer: react
Missing surface: three trailing parts of a web `ListRow`: an unread dot after its title, a tag before its end, and an action outside its press area.
Why Box/Stack/Inline/Card/Text/Button/Field cannot do this: every list on the training boards is a document row: its kind's mark, its title with a blue dot while the person has not opened it, a grey line, an "Only in English" tag when one language exists, and a ••• menu of the document's own commands. The row opens the document, so it is a `ListRow` link; the menu is a button, which cannot sit inside the link, and `ListRow` has no place for a second control, a dot or a tag. Composing the row by hand in Teisoro would copy ListRow's layout, naming and hover CSS.
Existing surface this might already be: `ListRow` (`leading`, `title`, `detail`, `href`, `valueBadge`); `valueBadge` draws a status, but as the row's value, read in its name and placed in the value column; a `Table` row with an action cell (a table, not a list).
Workaround I almost used: a `ListRow` without the menu and a separate menu column in a `Grid`, which breaks the row's hairlines and hover.
Teisoro use: `training/TrainingRow` in the Library's section cards, "New for you", the section pages and the phone lists (S04).
Proposed API (inventory): `unread?: boolean` (a dot after the title, read as `unreadLabel`), `tag?: ReactNode` (a Badge before the accessory), `action?: ReactNode` (a trailing control outside the row's press area).

## Accepted API (2026-10-19)

- `unread?: boolean` and `unreadLabel?: string`: while `unread`, an accent dot (`sw-list-row-unread`, a space-2 circle drawn as a border so forced colours keep it, in CanvasText) after the title's last word on its line, `aria-hidden`, and `unreadLabel` in a visually hidden span after the title. A pressable row's name is its title, the unread words, then its value ("Heron calls New"); a statement row reads the words after the title. `unread` without `unreadLabel`, or a blank `unreadLabel`, throws a `RangeError`, so the colour is never the only sign. A consumer's `aria-label` replaces the whole name.
- `tag?: ReactNode`: a short status (`sw-list-row-tag`), usually a `Badge`, after the value and before the chevron or check, centred on the row's height and never wrapped. A pressable row is described by it after its detail lines; `undefined`, `null` or a boolean (the `false` of `canArchive && <Badge …/>`) draws nothing, and the same holds for `action`.
- `action?: ReactNode`: a control (`sw-list-row-action`) after the row in the same list item (`sw-list-item-action`), a sibling of the row's button or link, so it is never inside the press area and a press on it never opens the row. It is centred on the row's height, after the row in the Tab order; the row ends a space-2 before it and it keeps the row's inline end padding after it (a space-4 in a panel, none in a plain list). Where `:has` is supported, the quiet hover fill spans the whole item while the row is hovered; elsewhere the row keeps its own fill. The consumer names it ("More for Heron calls") and pairs it with `accessory="none"` when a chevron would sit beside it. It works in a footer row too.
- A private `ListRowItem` renders the item (an `li` in the list, a `div` in a footer) with the action; the row's naming moved to `list/row-naming.ts`, and the new rules to `css/css-list-row-trailing.ts`.
- No new tokens, layers or dependencies. New classes `sw-list-row-unread`, `sw-list-row-tag`, `sw-list-item-action`, `sw-list-row-action`.

### Differences from the boards (Teisoro D-2)

- The dot is the theme's accent, a space-2 (8 px) circle; the boards' is a fixed blue 7 px dot.
- The ••• control is the consumer's `ActionMenu` with its 44 px coarse-pointer target; the boards draw a bare glyph.
