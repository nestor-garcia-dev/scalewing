Scalewing request from FutMas.

Status: implemented for `@scalewing/react` 1.59.0 (accepted 2026-10-25; FutMas F-010-S22 "Spanish, keyboard and screen-reader audit", Scalewing gaps 1 to 4; standing owner approval).
Renderer: react
Missing surface: muted words that keep 4.5:1 on a pressable row's hover fill, a live region primitive that announces reliably, a skip link in `AppShell`, and a disabled `Select`. A fix to `FixtureRow`, `ListRow` and `TableRow pressable`, a new `LiveRegion`, and extensions of `AppShell` and `Select`.
Why Box/Stack/Inline/Card/Text/Button/Field cannot do this: the hover fill and the muted colour are Scalewing's, and the consumer's guard forbids overriding styles; `Text aria-live` mounted with its words is often not read and cannot repeat the same words, and Scalewing has no visually hidden text for apps; `AppShell` puts 10 to 16 destinations before `main` on every page with no way to add a first link before them (`SiteHeader` has `skipLink`, `AppShell` did not); `Select` has no `disabled`, so a control that does nothing still looks and reads as usable.
Existing surface this might already be: dropping the hover fill (loses the pressable cue); `Text as="span" aria-live="polite"` filled after mount (seven hand-rolled spots in FutMas); landmarks alone for the shell (axe's `bypass` passes, keyboard users still Tab through the sidebar); ignoring a change while a write is in flight.
Workaround I almost used: the four fallbacks in the S22 plan (a known issue for the owner review, the hand-rolled `aria-live` spans, landmarks only, and S16's ignored change with a "Saving…" line).

## Accepted API (2026-10-25)

- Hover contrast (gap 1): no new prop. While a pressable `FixtureRow` (and a `detail` under one with an `action` or `select`, which the fill spans), a pressable `ListRow` (hovered or pressed) or a `TableRow pressable` (hovered, pressed, or its action focused) shows the quiet fill, the parts on it read `--sw-color-muted` as `--sw-color-text` (`mutedReadsAsText` in `css/quiet-fill.ts`), and a muted `TableCell`'s own colour is the text colour. Muted measures 4.14:1 on the fill in `ink` light and as low as 3.47:1 (`harvest` dark); the text colour keeps 4.5:1 there in every palette and scheme over the page, a surface and the grouped canvas (`quiet-fill-ink.test.ts`). The proposal's "switch to the token that keeps 4.5:1" is this, through the token, so a consumer's `Text color="muted"` in a slot follows too. Toned words keep their tone: no single mix keeps success, danger and accent at 4.5:1 on the fill in every palette (danger already falls under at rest in `mocha` dark), recorded as a follow-up. Forced colours unchanged (the `Highlight` outline). Gallery e2e hovers each row and checks contrast and axe in `ink` light and `harvest`, `mocha` and `sunburst` dark.
- `LiveRegion` (gap 2): `children?: string`, `politeness?: 'polite' | 'assertive'` (`liveRegionPolitenesses`, default polite), `announceKey?: string | number`, `visuallyHidden?: boolean`, `variant?` and `color?` (as `Text`'s, not with `visuallyHidden`), `id?`. An always-mounted `span`, empty at first (`role="status"` or `"alert"`, `aria-live`, `aria-atomic="true"`), whose words are a keyed child swapped in on each change of the words or the key. Refined from the proposal: `children` is a string (a status line's words), and the first words wait until the region has been mounted 100 ms, since screen readers miss words added as a region appears. Unknown politeness throws a `RangeError`; a look with `visuallyHidden` a `TypeError`. `ScheduleGrid` keeps its own region for now.
- `AppShell` `skipLink?: { label: string }` (`AppShellSkipLink`) (gap 3): the shell's first focusable element, `SiteHeader`'s skip link look (now shared, `css/skip-link.ts`) fixed at the window's top start on a new `skipLink` stacking layer over the top chrome. With it `main` gets an id and `tabindex="-1"`; a press focuses `main` without following the fragment (the address and history are unchanged) and `main` takes no ring. Opt-in rather than built in, so existing shells gain no English Tab stop. As proposed otherwise.
- `Select` `disabled?: boolean` (gap 4): the trigger is a native disabled button (out of the Tab order, read as dimmed, faded, `GrayText` in forced colours), the list cannot open (an open one closes) and `onChange` is never called. As proposed.
