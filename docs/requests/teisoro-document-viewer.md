Scalewing request from Teisoro.

Status: released in `@scalewing/react` 1.42.0 (PR #201; accepted 2026-10-19; Teisoro F-014-S03 "Scalewing: file picker, PDF viewer, Markdown view, blue count", task 2610; the training library inventory's row 12 and §2; plan approval under the owner's F-014 delegation of 2026-10-19).
Renderer: react
Missing surface: `DocumentViewer`, a PDF drawn one page at a time from its bytes.
Why Box/Stack/Inline/Card/Text/Button/Field cannot do this: the training readers show the provider's compliance PDFs inside Teisoro, on a counter computer, a tablet and a phone: one page at a time with 48-point page arrows, "Page 1 of 2", fit width, pinch to zoom, pages drawn as they arrive, and "This PDF couldn't be shown" with Try again and Download PDF. A browser's own PDF viewer (an `iframe` or `object`) needs a URL, draws its own toolbar with download and print, behaves differently on every phone (iOS shows the first page only, many Android browsers download instead), and cannot be styled. Drawing pages needs a PDF renderer.
Existing surface this might already be: none. `Pagination` steps through pages of a list, not a document's pages.
Workaround I almost used: an `iframe` of a blob URL, which drops the reader on phones and puts the browser's download and print in front of cashiers.
Teisoro use: the PDF reader (desktop and phone) in F-014-S04.
Proposed API (inventory): takes the PDF bytes, not a URL; no download or print of its own.

## New production dependency: `pdfjs-dist` 6.4.299 (exact)

- **Why:** drawing a PDF needs a full PDF parser and renderer; writing one is out of the question. PDF.js (Mozilla, Apache-2.0) is the browser-grade, maintained renderer that Firefox ships, with a text layer for assistive technology and selection. Teisoro's API opens the same files with the same library to prove they open and count their pages (Teisoro F-014 D-17 and D-32), so the server and the viewer agree on what opens.
- **Version:** 6.4.299, published 2026-10-03, the newest stable release at least two weeks old on 2026-10-19, pinned exactly; it is the version Teisoro's API pins (Teisoro F-014 D-32), so one PDF.js serves both.
- **Weight and loading:** the viewer imports PDF.js with a dynamic `import()` the first time it opens a document, so a page without a viewer, and every app that never renders one, downloads none of it. The worker (about 1.3 MB minified) is a separate module worker the consumer bundles from `@scalewing/react/document-viewer-worker`, Scalewing's entry for the same pinned version, so the viewer and its worker can never differ (PDF.js refuses a worker of another version). PDF.js's optional `@napi-rs/canvas` is for Node only and is never loaded in a browser; it still installs (28 to 35 MB per platform) unless the consumer skips it, as Teisoro does with pnpm's `ignoredOptionalDependencies` (Teisoro F-014 D-32). `pdfjs-dist` asks for Node 22.13 or later (`engines`), Teisoro's floor since D-32 and within Scalewing's.
- **Build:** PDF.js's legacy build, because the modern one needs `Map.prototype.getOrInsertComputed` and other features newer than the browsers stores run (the Chromium Playwright ships failed to open a PDF with it). The legacy build carries its own polyfills.
- **Hardening:** `isEvalSupported: false` (PDF.js 6 removed the option; its bundles contain no `eval` or `new Function`, and the option is still passed so the settings read as the API's), `useWorkerFetch: false`, `useWasm: false`, no `cMapUrl`, `standardFontDataUrl`, `wasmUrl` or `iccUrl` (nothing is fetched beyond the bytes; PDF.js falls back to system fonts and skips JPX images and colour profiles it cannot decode), `enableXfa: false`, `disableRange`, `disableStream` and `disableAutoFetch` true, no annotation layer (links in a PDF do nothing), no scripting, `verbosity: 0`. The bytes are copied before PDF.js transfers them to its worker, so the consumer's bytes stay usable for a download.

## Accepted API (2026-10-19)

- `DocumentViewer` (`DocumentViewerProps`, `DocumentViewerLabels`):
  - `data: ArrayBuffer | Uint8Array`, the PDF's bytes; new bytes open again;
  - `workerSrc: string`, the URL of the bundled worker entry (Vite: `import workerSrc from '@scalewing/react/document-viewer-worker?worker&url'`);
  - `label: string`, the viewer's name (the document's title);
  - `labels`: `previous`, `next`, `page(page, pageCount)`, `loading`, `failed` and optional `failedDetail`, all the consumer's words;
  - `page?` and `onPageChange?(page)`, the page held by the consumer or by the viewer; out of range shows the nearest;
  - `failedAction?: ReactNode`, the failed state's actions (Try again, Download PDF);
  - `onLoad?({ pageCount })` and `onError?(error)`.
- A `section` (the one region landmark) named by `label`; once open, its stage is a focusable group named by the page words: a quiet grey stage at the large radius, the page centred with a space-5 margin (space-3 below `md`) at the stage's width (fit width), on the surface with the small elevation until PDF.js paints it; the page box takes the page's shape as soon as it is known, and the page is drawn into it as PDF.js paints, at the screen's pixel ratio (capped at 16 megapixels).
- The page's text is PDF.js's text layer over the drawing, transparent, read by assistive technology and selectable (a selection tinted with the accent); the canvas is hidden from assistive technology.
- Under the page, `labels.page` in a polite, atomic live region, between 48-point round neutral steps named `labels.previous` and `labels.next` (the pager's private chevron); at either end a step is `aria-disabled` and stays a Tab stop; with one page only the words show.
- Zoom: two fingers zoom the page between fit width and four times as wide, keeping the spot of the page between them under the fingers (the page's place is read before and after the change, the stage's padding included; the stage scrolls across and the boxes around it, the window at last, scroll down); the stage allows only panning (`touch-action: pan-x pan-y`), so the screen does not zoom instead, and a zoomed page scrolls sideways inside the stage, never the page. `+` (or `=`), `-` and `0` on the focused stage zoom by a quarter and back to fit width, about the middle of the stage's visible part. The page is stretched while zooming and drawn again at its size 150 ms after the zoom rests. Another page opens at fit width.
- Loading: `labels.loading` as a Spinner status, its visible words hidden from assistive technology so they are heard once, in the text colour (4.5:1 on the stage). Failed (the bytes do not open, or a page cannot be drawn; a cancelled draw is not a failure): `labels.failed`, `labels.failedDetail` and `failedAction` in an alert, in the text colour, and `onError`. When only a page cannot be drawn, the steps and page words stay, so another page can be read. New bytes start at the first page.
- It has no download or print of its own and opens no URL.
- Missing or blank words, a blank `workerSrc`, or a `labels.page` that is not a function throw a `RangeError`.
- New export path `@scalewing/react/document-viewer-worker`; new classes `sw-document-viewer`, `-stage`, `-page`, `-text`, `-bar`, `-step`, `-status`, `-message`, `-actions`. No new tokens or layers. `useChartWidth` now uses a shared `useElementWidth`.

### Differences from the boards (Teisoro D-2)

- The stage is Scalewing's quiet grey fill and the page fills its width inside a space-5 margin; the boards draw a narrower page with wider margins on a fixed grey.
- The steps are the neutral grey Button; the boards draw a white "next" circle and a faded "previous".
- "Pages drawn as they arrive": the viewer receives the whole file as bytes (no URL, no range requests), so a page is shown as soon as the document opens, without waiting for the other pages, and is painted progressively as PDF.js draws it.
- The phone board's "pellizca para acercar" hint is the consumer's words in `labels.page`.
