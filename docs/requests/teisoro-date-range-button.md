Scalewing request from Teisoro.

Status: released in `@scalewing/react` 1.40.0 (PR #181; accepted 2026-10-14; Teisoro F-012-S05, release M3 "periods, lists and figures", item 1; the manager views inventory's row 4 and risk 3; plan approval under Teisoro decisions O-1, D-1 and D-9).
Renderer: react
Missing surface: `DateRangeButton`, a button showing a range of days that opens a calendar to pick a first and a last day, with presets.
Why Box/Stack/Inline/Card/Text/Button/Field cannot do this: every report's period control has a date button ("Oct 6 – 12, 2026") between ‹ and ›. The PeriodPicker board opens a popover from it: presets on the left (Last 7 days, This month, Last month, Last 90 days, This year, Last year), then two months, then the chosen range with its length and how to pick, and Cancel and Show. The PhonePeriod board is a bottom sheet: the title, the range and its length under it, the presets in a row, one month, the hint, and Cancel and Show. A range is up to a year (the store's reports read up to 366 days), and days after today cannot be picked. Teisoro would otherwise rebuild the calendar grid, its keyboard, its popover and its sheet.
Existing surface this might already be: `CalendarButton` picks one day; its `range` prop only tints the period around that day. `DateField` is typed entry of one day.
Workaround I almost used: two `CalendarButton`s, one for each end, which cannot show the range being picked, cannot limit its length, and cannot offer presets.
Teisoro use: `PeriodControl` (F-012 composite, grown from `reports/PeriodToolbar`) on every report.
Proposed API (inventory): `label`, `value: { start, end }`, `onChange`, `presets?: { id, label, range }[]`, `min`, `max`, `maxDays?`, `today`, `weekStartsOn`, `labels`. Two months from `md`, one month in a bottom sheet below. Reuses the private calendar grid.

## Decision: a separate component, not a `CalendarButton` mode (inventory risk 3)

`DateRangeButton` is its own component over the same private calendar pieces. A `mode` on `CalendarButton` would make its `value` and `onChange` a union (a day or a range), so every existing caller's types would widen, and the two differ in more than the value: CalendarButton is icon-only and picks on one press; DateRangeButton shows its range as words, picks in two presses, waits for Apply, offers presets, and becomes a bottom sheet on a phone. The shared parts are private and shared, not copied:

- `CalendarGrid` takes a range as `selected` (every day in it selected, the grid multiselectable), `latest` (the last day a press may pick, earlier than `max`) and `blankOutside` (months side by side show each day once). Its day-only use is unchanged.
- In `components/calendar/`: `CalendarGrid`, `CalendarPopover` (the anchored modal popover: Escape, Tab kept inside, a press outside), `useGridFocus` (focus follows the focus day), `wrapTab`, `MonthStep` and `sameDayIn` / `daysBetween` / `monthsBetween` in `calendar-month.ts` are split out of `CalendarDialog` and `CalendarHeader`, which now use them. The band's colours are one shared constant (`css/range-band.ts`). CalendarButton's and DateField's public behaviour and markup are unchanged; their tests pass untouched.

## Accepted API (2026-10-14)

- `DateRangeButton` (props `DateRangeButtonProps`; types `DateRangeButtonValue`, `DateRangePreset`, `DateRangeButtonLabels`), a forwarded ref to the `<button>`:
  - `label: string`, what pressing it does ("Choose a period"); it names the popover and titles the sheet.
  - `value: { start, end }` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, both included) and `onChange(value)`, called on Apply or a preset only when the range changed. A value outside `min`/`max` or longer than `maxDays` stays the value, as on CalendarButton.
  - `presets?: { id, label, range }[]`: a press applies its range at once and closes. The preset matching the range shown is `aria-pressed`. A preset outside `min`/`max` or longer than `maxDays`, a repeated or empty id, or an empty label throws; the consumer computes its presets from the same bounds and leaves out one that does not fit (Teisoro's `PeriodControl` drops, for example, Last year for a store opened this year).
  - `min?`, `max?` (days after it are disabled), `maxDays?` (a whole number from 1; once a first day is pressed, the days past this many are disabled), `today?` (the day marked; the device's by default), `weekStartsOn?` (0 or 1), `locale?` (the nearest `lang`, then en-US), `disabled?`, `id?`.
  - `labels?: Partial<DateRangeButtonLabels>`: `previousMonth`, `nextMonth`, `nameSeparator` (as CalendarButton's), `presets` (the group's name), `apply`, `cancel`, `close` (the sheet's close button), `hint` (how to pick) and `dayCount(days)` (the range's length, "7 days"). English defaults; Teisoro passes its own, "Show" for `apply`. An empty word throws.
  - `size?` (`xs`, `sm`, `md`; default `md`, 44 px on a coarse pointer at every size) and `variant?` (default `neutral`, the board's grey).
- The button shows a calendar glyph and the range in the locale, sharing what both ends have (`Intl.DateTimeFormat.formatRange`, or both ends in full where a browser lacks it: "Oct 6 – 12, 2026", "6–12 oct 2026", one day as one date). Its name is `label`, `nameSeparator`, then that range ("Choose a period, Oct 6 – 12, 2026"), so the words shown are in the name. It has `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` while open.
- Picking: the first press sets the start (a one-day range waiting for its end), the second sets the end. A second press before the start starts over from that day; a press on a whole range starts a new one. Apply with only a start applies that one day. The days of the range are `aria-selected` (the grids are `aria-multiselectable`), its ends drawn as the selected circles and the days between as CalendarButton's band. The range being picked and its length are a polite, atomic status, so each press is heard; one polite region says the months shown when they change.
- Keyboard: as CalendarButton's grid (arrows, Home, End, PageUp, PageDown, with Shift by year, Enter and Space pick), one roving day across both months, the months following it. Escape cancels and returns focus; Tab stays inside the popover.
- From `md` up: a popover on the top layer beside the button (DateField's calendar surface): the presets, two months (the range's end in the second, never a month before `min`'s first), and a footer with the range in the label weight, its length and the hint in the muted caption, then Cancel (`neutral`) and Apply (`primary`), `sm` (44 px on a coarse pointer). From `lg` (64rem) the presets are a list beside the months; from `md` to `lg`, where two 44 px months and a list do not fit side by side, the same list wraps in a row above the months. Cancel, Escape and a press outside keep the value.
- Below `md`: a `Dialog` bottom sheet (`sheetBelow="md"`) titled by `label`, with the range and its length as its description, the presets as a row of pills that scrolls sideways (the pressed one in the text colour), one month, and a footer with the hint over Cancel and Apply (Apply twice as wide). Its close button, Escape and Cancel keep the value. The presentation follows the width while open, keeping the range being picked.
- Focus returns to the button after the calendar closes (after the sheet has left the page, which is inert while it is modal).
- `ButtonGroup attached` finds its buttons by type (`~`, `:first-of-type`, `:last-of-type`), so an open calendar beside a button in the group keeps the next button's divider and the last one's corners. Its rendering is otherwise unchanged.
- No new tokens, layers or dependencies. New classes `sw-date-range-button`, `-popover`, `-body`, `-months`, `-month`, `-month-header`, `-month-title`, `-footer`, `-summary`, `-status`, `-line`, `-count`, `-separator`, `-hint`, `-actions`, `-sheet`, `-sheet-footer`, `-presets`, `-presets-row` and `-preset` (all `sw-date-range-*`). The popover reuses `sw-date-field-calendar`, so it is on the `popup` layer and opaque under reduced transparency.

### Differences from the boards (Teisoro D-2)

- The boards' days are 36 px; Scalewing's are DateField's 44 px squares, so from `md` to `lg` the presets sit above the months.
- The boards' weekday headers are one letter; Scalewing's are the locale's short names, as in DateField.
- The board's band runs behind the ends' circles; Scalewing's ends are the selected circle and the band starts beside them, as CalendarButton's tint.
- The boards show no month steps; Scalewing adds ‹ before the first month and › after the last.
- On the phone the sheet's close button takes the focus on open, as every Dialog does.
