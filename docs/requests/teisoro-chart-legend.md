Scalewing request from Teisoro.

Status: accepted 2026-10-13 for `@scalewing/react` 1.39.0 (Teisoro F-012-S04, release M2 "charts", item 1; the manager views inventory's row 12; plan approval under Teisoro decisions O-1 and D-1).
Renderer: react
Missing surface: `ChartLegend`, a chart's key: a list of swatches (a bar, a line, a marker, the boxed current period), each beside its words, in the charts' own colours.
Why Box/Stack/Inline/Card/Text/Button/Field cannot do this: every chart board (ReportsOverview "Off, last 13 weeks" and "How the cash moved", Registers, Services, Corrections, Vault, and their phone boards) draws a key above the chart: a small square in each series' colour, a short line for the running total, a red downward triangle for a money truck, and a dashed outline for "This week". A swatch is a coloured mark with no text, which Teisoro cannot draw (no CSS, no inline style), and its colour must be the one the chart draws for that series, which only Scalewing knows.
Existing surface this might already be: `Badge` (words in a pill, not a key); `GlyphTile` (a categorical square holding a glyph, a tile, not a swatch beside words).
Workaround I almost used: a `Box` with an inline background hex per item.
Teisoro use: `RunningTotalChart` (the 13-week charts), the Vault's line charts, and `Statement` (the key above the statement's range bars).
Proposed API (inventory): `ChartLegend` with `items: {label, swatch: 'bar' | 'line' | 'marker' | 'box', tone}[]`, an accessible list, shared by `BarChart`, `ColumnChart`, `LineChart` and `DataBar` users.

## Accepted API (2026-10-13)

- `ChartLegend` (props `ChartLegendProps`: a `ul`'s attributes but `children`, plus `items: readonly ChartLegendItem[]`), a forwarded ref. `ChartLegendItem` is `{ label: string; swatch: 'bar'; tone: ChartFillTone }` or `{ label: string; swatch: 'line' | 'marker' | 'box'; tone: ChartStrokeTone }`. Exported with `ChartLegendSwatch` and `chartLegendSwatches`, `ChartTone`, `ChartFillTone` and `chartFillTones`, `ChartStrokeTone` and `chartStrokeTones`.
- One tone table for every chart (`src/css/chart-tones.ts`), so a swatch is always the colour of the mark it names. A tone class, `sw-chart-tone-<tone>`, hands its roles to the marks inside it as custom properties:
  - `--sw-chart-fill` (a column, a range bar, a `bar` swatch): the categorical hues mixed 72 % into the surface for a chart's series (lighter, so a line drawn over them stands apart), and `DataBar`'s statement tones (`neutral` muted 65 %, `success` 55 %, `muted` 35 %, `strong` text 85 %, `accent`; `danger` at 40 % for a shortage was added by `teisoro-data-bar-short.md`). `neutral` is one grey for both. Every fill keeps 1.5:1 on the surface in every palette and scheme.
  - `--sw-chart-stroke` (a line, a marker, the boxed period): the semantic colours `accent`, `success`, `warning`, `danger`, and `neutral` as the muted colour. Every stroke keeps 3:1 on the surface (WCAG 1.4.11). The categorical hues are not strokes: in the darker palettes they fall under 3:1.
  - `--sw-chart-ink` (the words beside a mark: a callout, a line's last value, a marker's label): a hue moved 70 % toward the text colour, a semantic colour 50 %, muted for neutral. Every ink keeps 4.5:1 on the surface.
  - `chart-tones.test.ts` holds each role to its contrast in every palette and scheme.
- `DataBar`'s fills now come from that table (`chartFill`); its generated CSS is unchanged.
- Swatches: a `bar` is a spacing step 3 square in the fill; a `line` is a spacing step 4 long and 2 px thick (the charts' line width, `src/css/chart-marks.ts`) in the stroke; a `marker` is a spacing step 3 downward triangle (`clip-path`) in the stroke, the shape a chart's marker takes; a `box` is a spacing step 4 by 3 outline, a 1 px dashed stroke with half the `sm` radius. A `bar` takes only a fill tone and the others only a stroke tone; any other swatch or tone, or an empty or blank `label`, throws a `RangeError`.
- Layout: a `ul` in a wrapping row (`column-gap` spacing step 5, `row-gap` step 2), the caption type in the text colour; each item its swatch and its words with a spacing step 2 gap, the swatch centred on the words' first line (a margin from the caption's line height) however many lines they wrap to. The contrasts hold on the surface, so a chart and its legend sit on it (a `Card`).
- Accessibility: every swatch is `aria-hidden`, so an item reads as its words; the list keeps `role="list"` (Safari drops the role of a list with no markers) and is named with `aria-label`. In forced colours every swatch is `CanvasText`.
- No new tokens, layers or dependencies. New classes `sw-chart-legend`, `sw-chart-legend-item`, `sw-chart-legend-swatch`, `sw-chart-legend-<swatch>` and `sw-chart-tone-<tone>`.
- The tone table stays in the renderer, as DataBar's fills did (Teisoro D-39): it maps tokens to CSS `color-mix` expressions and adds no token. Moving the shares into `@scalewing/tokens` (review round one's suggestion) is a tokens release, which this release is not cleared to make; it is left for a native chart that needs them.
- Review round one (fresh Bar Raiser) centred the swatch on the first line of wrapped words, refused an empty label, pinned DataBar's rules literally, showed every series hue and the quieter lines in the gallery, and renamed the membership check `isOneOf` with an explicit ink tone list.
- `BarChart` is unchanged: its default bar is a `bar` swatch in `accent`; its toned bars are the full semantic colour, the `line` swatch's colour. Its tones were not moved onto the shared table, which would lighten its existing bars.

### Differences from the boards (Teisoro D-2)

- The boards' swatches are hexes (series tints such as #8fb3d9 and #c7a4dc, the statement greys and green, #0066cc for the line and box, #c4291c for the truck marker). Scalewing draws them from the theme, so they follow dark mode and the named palettes; the series tints are a little stronger than the board's (72 % of the categorical hue) to keep 1.5:1 on a dark surface.
- The board's bar swatch is 10 px with 2 px corners, its line swatch 14 by 3 px, its marker the ▼ glyph at 11 px and its box 14 by 10 px; Scalewing's are a spacing step 3 (12 px) square with 2 px corners, a 16 by 2 px line, a 12 px triangle and a 16 by 12 px box. The gap between items is a spacing step 5 (24 px; the board's is 20 px).
- The board's two registers are two blues; the categorical scale has one blue, so Teisoro takes two hues (for example blue and teal).
