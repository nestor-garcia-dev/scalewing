Scalewing request from Teisoro.

Status: released in `@scalewing/react` 1.32.0 (PR #142; accepted 2026-10-11; Teisoro F-011 release R6 "canvas fidelity"; plan approval under Teisoro decisions O-1 to O-4, D-1, D-2).
Renderer: react
Missing surface: `DenominationGrid` grid-level appearance options for a framed count sheet: `frame`, `totalEmphasis`, `cellToneAppearance`, and an empty `zeroLabel`. An extension of `DenominationGrid` (`teisoro-denomination-grid.md`, `teisoro-denomination-grid-label.md`), not a new component.
Why Box/Stack/Inline/Card/Text/Button/Field cannot do this: the F-011 canvas draws every count grid (the drawer sheets DrawerAdd, DrawerAudit, DrawerAdjust, DrawerDrop; VaultCount1; PosClose1; AuditReview1/2; 64 boards in all) as a framed table: a white surface inside a hairline with rounded corners, a grey heading row, a rule between columns, blank cells where a count is zero, bold totals in the text color, and a difference (or a changed "after" count) on a tinted pill. `DenominationGrid` draws open rows with muted dashes for zeros, small muted totals and a tone that colors only the figure, and `zeroLabel=""` throws. Teisoro owns no CSS (its AGENTS.md), so the look must come from Scalewing, and FutMas and fantasy-football must keep theirs.
Existing surface this might already be: `Table` (`columnRules` draws rules but is not a count grid with entry rows); `labelAppearance="plain"` (labels only); row `emphasis` (a row's fill).
Workaround I almost used: wrapping the grid in a Card (no heading fill or column rules) and passing `zeroLabel=" "` (a blank that still reads as a space).
Teisoro use: the `BillCount` composite and every read-only count grid on the redesign.
Proposed API: `frame?: 'none' | 'table'`, `totalEmphasis?: 'default' | 'strong'`, `cellToneAppearance?: 'text' | 'pill'`, and `zeroLabel=""` allowed. Generic names: how a grid sits on the page, how its totals read, how a toned count shows.

## Accepted API (2026-10-11)

- `frame?: DenominationGridFrame` (`'none' | 'table'`, default `none`; `denominationGridFrames`). `table`: the scroll region takes the surface and a `--sw-color-border` hairline at `--sw-radius-sm` (the board's 10 px is between `sm` 8 and `md` 16; D-2 keeps the scale), the heading row the subtle fill, every cell after a row's first a border rule, the counts label type centred under their heads, cells padded space-3 across (space-2 below `md`) and space-4 down (the heading row space-2). Entry cells keep their own space-1 padding; a quiet row's pinned label keeps its fill. The frame is the region's border, so a wide strip scrolls inside it and the scroll shades still draw. The tiles layout ignores it, as it ignores `labelWidth` and `totalLabel`.
- `totalEmphasis?: DenominationTotalEmphasis` (`'default' | 'strong'`, default `default`; `denominationTotalEmphases`). `strong`: label type (15/20) at weight 700; a neutral row's total in the text color, a toned row's in its tone (the board's red difference total); the phone's inline total too.
- `cellToneAppearance?: DenominationCellToneAppearance` (`'text' | 'pill'`, default `text`; `denominationCellToneAppearances`). `pill`: a toned read-only strip count sits on `toneTintFill` (the tone mixed 10 % into the surface; the accent tint for `accent`) in `toneTintInk` (the tone mixed 70 % with the text color) at `--sw-radius-sm`, the Notice's own fill and words, whose words keep 4.5:1 on the tint in every palette except where a palette's own danger is already under 4.5:1 on its surface (harvest and mocha dark), where it is no worse than the bare tone, as `notice.test.tsx` checks; the blue "after" highlight is an `accent` tone. Forced colors outline the pill in CanvasText. Every read-only strip count now sits in a `sw-denomination-cell-value` span, which the pill fills; an empty value draws no pill.
- `zeroLabel=""` leaves zero and empty read-only counts blank (the column header still names the cell); a non-string `zeroLabel` throws a `RangeError` (an empty string threw before).
- An unknown `frame`, `totalEmphasis` or `cellToneAppearance` throws a `RangeError`.
- Shared tint: the Notice's tint and ink moved from `css-notice.ts` to `css/tone-tint.ts` (`toneTintFill`, `toneTintInk`), so the Notice and the pill share one rule; the Notice's CSS is unchanged.
- Sizes (D-2): heading row about 34 px (board 36), read-only rows 52 px (board 52 to 58), entry rows the Stepper's 44 plus padding (board 56 to 60).
- Generated classes: `sw-denomination-scroll-framed`, `sw-denomination-framed`, `sw-denomination-totals-strong`, `sw-denomination-cell-tones-pill`, `sw-denomination-cell-value`; rules in `css-denomination-appearance.ts` (frame, totals) and `css-denomination-cell-tones.ts` (pill).

Evidence: `denomination-appearance.test.tsx` (defaults unchanged, the frame classes, tiles unframed, blank zeros in both layouts, strong totals on both layouts, the value span and pill class, the entry limit appearance passed to every Stepper, unknown values throw, the generated rules, their order after the label appearances and narrow padding, the shared tint with the Notice); `denomination-grid.test.tsx` (the per-tone fill and ink variables, a non-string zero label throws); gallery "DenominationGrid" example "Wading birds at the weir"; `apps/gallery/e2e/denomination-framed.spec.ts` on desktop-en, mobile-es and forced-colors (the frame, the heading fill and column rule, blank zeros, bold text-color totals, a danger and an accent pill at 4.5:1 or more, an outlined pill in forced colors, − unfaded at zero and enabled after +).
