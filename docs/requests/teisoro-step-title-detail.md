Scalewing request from Teisoro.

Status: released in `@scalewing/react` 1.45.0 (PR #220; accepted 2026-10-20; Teisoro F-014-S06 "Acceptance and UX check", task 2628; the fidelity gap its review found on the training boards TrainingAdd and TrainingWrite; plan approval under the owner's F-014 delegation of 2026-10-19).
Renderer: react
Missing surface: muted words after an open `Step`'s title, on the title's line.
Why Box/Stack/Inline/Card/Text/Button/Field cannot do this: Teisoro's add and edit forms are `Steps open` (its `GuidedForm`). The boards draw muted words beside some steps' titles: "A note" with "Optional" (TrainingAdd), "The page" with "English done · Español empty" (TrainingWrite), a 14 px regular muted line on the title's baseline 8 px after it. `Step` takes its `title` as a string and draws the header itself; its only muted words are a skipped step's `skippedReason`, which shows on skipped steps only and is part of the heading. Teisoro owns no CSS and cannot place words in `Step`'s header row.
Existing surface this might already be: `Step` `skippedReason` (muted words after the title, skipped steps only, inside the heading); `Step` `summary` (a done step's folded line).
Workaround I almost used: putting the words in the step's body above the fields, which moves them off the title's line and adds a row the boards do not have.
Teisoro use: `GuidedForm`'s sections in Training's add and edit forms (Optional on the note step; each language's state on the page step), passing catalog words.
Proposed API: `titleDetail?: string` on `Step`, opt-in.

## Accepted API (2026-10-20)

- `titleDetail?: string`: drawn while the step is open (current or error, or every step of `Steps open`, done ones included, with fields or without); a folded step draws its summary or reason instead. A blank one throws a `RangeError`, as a blank `skippedReason` does.
- Look: the label size (15 px) and letter spacing at the body's weight (400) in `--sw-color-muted`, a skipped step's reason's colour (not its size: the reason is part of the title and takes its size and weight; these words are a note beside it). The title and the words share a wrapping line (`sw-steps-title-line`: flex, baseline-aligned, a space-2 apart), so the words sit on the title's baseline after it, or on a line of their own under the title, at its start, where they do not fit (a long title at 390 px), never past the panel. The line gives way to a done step's summary and Edit as the title did. Scalewing draws only the gap; the consumer writes any separators inside the words ("English done · Español empty"), as with `skippedReason`. The boards draw no dot between title and words.
- Outside the heading, unlike `skippedReason`: the reason replaces the status word (it is the step's state, so it is in the heading's name), while these words are a note on the step. Keeping them out keeps the heading's name the title (with its status word, "A note, Done") and a list of headings readable; an open list's region keeps the title as its name and is described by the words (`aria-describedby`), read as a person enters it; in a stepped list they follow the heading in reading order. The boards do the same (the words beside the `h2`, the section named by the `h2`).
- The line is rendered whenever the step has `titleDetail`, so the heading keeps its element (and its focus) as the step opens and folds; a step without it renders exactly as before.
- Size: the boards draw the words at 14 px; Scalewing has no 14 px type step, and the nearest token is the label size, 15 px (caption is 13 px). The words take the token, as Teisoro's fidelity rules already take Scalewing's sizes over a board's fixed pixels (Teisoro F-014 D-132, D-134); for Teisoro's S06 review to record.
- Give a step its `titleDetail` from its first render: setting or clearing it later moves the heading into or out of the title line, which remounts it.
- A long word in the words breaks (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`) rather than pass the panel.
- New classes `sw-steps-title-line` and `sw-steps-title-detail` (`css/css-steps-title-detail.ts`); the step's prop checks move to `steps/assert-step.ts` and the title line to `steps/StepTitleLine.tsx`. No new tokens, layers or dependencies. Forced colors draw the words in the system text colour.

Evidence: `steps-title-detail.test.tsx` (an open list's words beside the title and outside the heading, the region described by them, the heading named by the title and its word; a stepped list draws them only while current or error, with the same heading element through every status; a step without them unchanged; blank words refused; the generated rules and the catalog); gallery "Steps" (a field-notes form with every step open: a done step with its words and summary, a long title with "Wetlands done · Woodlands empty", "A note" with "Optional"); `apps/gallery/e2e/steps-title-detail.spec.ts` on desktop-en, mobile-es and forced-colors (the regions' names and descriptions, the headings' names, the words outside the headings, 15 px at 400 in the muted token, "Optional" after "A note" on its line, the long title's words beside it at 1280 and under it at its start on a phone, inside the panel, no sideways scroll, the words kept as the step turns done, no axe violations), with `steps.spec.ts`, `steps-open.spec.ts` and `steps-status-words.spec.ts` unchanged and passing.
