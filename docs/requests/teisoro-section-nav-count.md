Scalewing request from Teisoro.

Status: released in `@scalewing/react` 1.30.0 (PR #134; Teisoro F-011-S05, release R4 "ledger and navigation", item 16; the redesign inventory's row 3). Extends `SectionNav` (`teisoro-section-nav.md`).
Renderer: react
Missing surface: `SectionNav` item counts and a filled appearance.
Why Box/Stack/Inline/Card/Text/Button/Field cannot do this: the redesign's sub-navigation (about 80 boards; the "Tasks 3" count on 18 Vault boards) is an area's name at 19 px with its pages as quiet buttons on the right, the current one on a grey fill, and a red count pill after "Tasks". `SectionNav` marks the current item with an underline or a start bar and has no count; a `Badge` beside a link is outside its accessible name, and Teisoro owns no CSS.
Existing surface this might already be: `SectionNav` (underline or side list), `Nav`, `SegmentedControl` (a choice, not links), `Badge`.
Workaround I almost used: `Nav` with small `Button`s and a `Badge` next to Tasks.
Teisoro use: `AreaBar` (splitting `WorkspaceShell.tsx`), on every page.
Proposed API (inventory): `SectionNavItem.count?: number` (a Badge after the label, included in the accessible name); `appearance?: 'underline' | 'filled'`, `filled` painting the current item with a grey fill.

## Accepted API (2026-10-11)

- `SectionNavItem.count?: number`: a whole number from 0. Above 0 it is a pill after the label (`sw-section-nav-count`): the danger colour with `onDanger` figures (4.5:1 in every palette, tested), caption size at weight 700, 20 high (`space-5` less `space-1`) and at least 20 wide; a `CanvasText` edge in forced colours. The link's name reads "Tasks 3" (the pill is text in the link after a space). 0 or none shows no pill. There is no cap; a long number widens the link.
- `SectionNavItem.countLabel?: string` (added in review): the count in the consumer's words, such as "3 tasks to do", read in the link's name in place of the bare number ("Tasks 3 tasks to do"), the pill then `aria-hidden`; a blank `countLabel`, or one without `count`, throws. Teisoro should pass one, since a bare "3" does not say what waits.
- `SectionNav` `appearance?: SectionNavAppearance` (`'underline' | 'filled'`, default `underline`; `sectionNavAppearances` exported). `filled`: no hairline under the row and no underline or side-list bar; every link the `sm` control (32; 44 on a coarse pointer) with the `sm` radius and padding at weight 500 inside a 2 px transparent border; the current link on a soft fill (the muted colour at 18%) in the text colour at weight 600, so it reads apart from a hovered link (12%), and a 2 px `Highlight` edge in forced colours. It works in the row and the side list (no start bar there; the filled rules carry two classes, so they outrank the side list's by specificity, not source order).
- Failure boundary: a `count` that is negative, fractional or not a number, a blank `countLabel` or one without `count`, or an unknown `appearance`, throws a `RangeError`.
- Sizes and looks on Scalewing's scale (Teisoro D-2): links 32 high with 12 padding and the 8 radius (board 32, 12, 8); labels at the label size 15, weight 500 and muted, the current one 600 in the text colour (board 14, 500 in #3a3a3c, the current one 600); the current fill is muted at 18% (board #e8e8ed); the count pill is caption 13 at 700 (board 12 at 700) on the theme's danger (board #ff3b30), 20 high with `space-1` (4) padding (board 6); the gap between the label and the pill is `space-2` (8; board 6) and between links `space-2` (board 4). The area's name at 19 px is the consumer's `Text` beside the nav (not part of `SectionNav`).
- Generated classes `sw-section-nav-filled`, `sw-section-nav-count`.

Evidence: `section-nav-filled.test.tsx` (the pill and the link's name, a `countLabel` read instead of the number, no pill at 0, the classes, failures, the danger contrast in every palette, the generated rules including weights, the coarse-pointer target, forced colours and the two-class specificity); gallery "SectionNav" (a count named in words on the underline side list, "Reserve pages" filled beside the area's name with Tasks 3, "Reserve archive" filled as a side list from md); `apps/gallery/e2e/section-nav.spec.ts` on desktop-en, mobile-es and forced-colors (the current page, the link named "Tasks 3", no hairline, 32 high or 44 on a phone, the current fill or forced-colours edge apart from the others, the pill's forced-colours edge, a 20-high pill; on desktop the filled side list's current link with the fill, no accent start bar and weight 600 against 500; "Rangers 2 walks to assign").
