Scalewing request from Teisoro.

Status: released in `@scalewing/react` 1.38.0 (PR #172; a danger tone for a shortage follows in `teisoro-data-bar-short.md`; accepted 2026-10-13; Teisoro F-012-S03, release M1 "tables that hold a period", item 3; the manager views inventory's rows 6 and 7 and its token-gap table; plan approval under Teisoro decisions O-1 and D-1).
Renderer: react
Missing surface: `DataBar`, a horizontal range bar for a table cell or a list row, from one value to another on a domain, with a zero line when the domain spans zero.
Why Box/Stack/Inline/Card/Text/Button/Field cannot do this: the statement boards (ReportsOverview "How the cash moved", Services, Vault, Bounced checks, a day, a bounced check) draw a bar in each line from where the running total stood to where the line leaves it, coloured by what the line is (where it started, came in, went out, expected, counted or recorded); the Overview's Days table draws each day's Off as a bar either side of a zero line beside its figure. `BarChart` is a whole labelled chart (its own list, labels, values and axis), not a cell's mark, and its bars always start at zero or the middle. A bar placed by shares needs an inline position and width, which Teisoro cannot write (no CSS, no inline style).
Existing surface this might already be: `BarChart` (a chart of magnitudes with an axis; `diverging`); `Progress` (a value toward a maximum, a status with a role, not a range).
Workaround I almost used: a `Box` with an inline `width` percentage and a background hex per line.
Teisoro use: `Statement` (grown from `ui/MathTable`): every statement line; the Days table's Off column; the place cards.
Proposed API (inventory): `DataBar` with `from`, `to`, `min`, `max`, `tone: 'neutral' | 'success' | 'muted' | 'strong' | 'accent'`, decorative (`aria-hidden`; the amount is the cell's text). Cash out uses `muted`, never danger.

## Accepted API (2026-10-13)

- `DataBar` (props `DataBarProps`: a `span`'s attributes but `children` and `aria-hidden`, plus `from: number`, `to: number`, `min: number`, `max: number`, `tone?: DataBarTone`, default `'neutral'`), a forwarded ref. Exported with `DataBarTone` and `dataBarTones` (`['neutral', 'success', 'muted', 'strong', 'accent']`).
- Geometry is a pure function in `src/data-bar-geometry.ts` (`dataBarGeometry`): the bar runs from the smaller of `from` and `to` to the larger, as shares of the domain; ends past the domain are clamped to it (a running total a cent past its domain from rounding stays on the track); a domain with `min < 0 < max` has a zero at its share. Non-finite numbers or `min >= max` throw a `RangeError`, as does an unknown tone. It stays in the renderer: it is a component's layout, not theme math, and the tokens package needs no change.
- The component hands the shares to generated CSS as unitless custom properties (`--sw-data-bar-start`, `--sw-data-bar-size`, `--sw-data-bar-zero`), the way `BarChart` hands its fill ratio. The bar is placed with logical insets, so a right-to-left table mirrors it.
- Look: it fills its container's width (a spacing step 6 at least), a spacing step 4 tall with no track fill, the bar's corners half the `sm` radius; a diverging domain (`sw-data-bar-diverging`) draws a 1 px border-colour hairline centred on zero, a spacing step 1 past the bar above and below, and the bar's end at zero is square (`sw-data-bar-zero-start` or `sw-data-bar-zero-end`, from the geometry's `zeroEnd`), as BarChart's diverging bars meet their line. In forced colours the bar and the line are `CanvasText`.
- Tones are semantic colours mixed into the surface (`dataBarToneMixes`, shared by the CSS and its test): `neutral` muted at 65 %, `success` success at 55 %, `muted` muted at 35 %, `strong` text at 85 %, `accent` the accent. The bar is decorative, so the bar need not meet a text contrast; the test keeps every tone at 1.5:1 or more on the surface and the three greys (out, start, subtotal) 1.5:1 or more from the next one in every palette and scheme. There is no danger tone: money out is `muted`, never red (the boards' ledger rule).
- Accessibility: `aria-hidden="true"` always (not overridable); a cell holding a bar and its figure is named by the figure alone.
- No new tokens, layers or dependencies. New classes `sw-data-bar`, `sw-data-bar-fill`, `sw-data-bar-diverging`, `sw-data-bar-zero-start`, `sw-data-bar-zero-end` and `sw-data-bar-<tone>`.
- A bar in its own column reads as empty cells to assistive technology while the figure's cell carries the value (the README says so); a bar and its figure can share one cell.
- Review round one (fresh Bar Raiser) made `aria-hidden` win over a spread, squared the zero end, centred the zero line, split the geometry tests, showed a clamped value and checked forced colours against `CanvasText`.

### Differences from the boards (Teisoro D-2)

- The boards' fills are hexes (start #8e8e93, in #7cc492, out #c3c8d1, expected #5e5e63, counted #0066cc; the Days column's lighter tints). Scalewing draws them from the theme: muted 65 %, success 55 %, muted 35 %, text 85 % and the accent over the surface, so the greys sit a little apart from the board's to stay distinct in every palette, and the bars follow dark mode and the named palettes.
- The Days board draws a short day's bar in a light red tint (#f4c7c2). DataBar has no danger tone, by the request; Teisoro draws a short day's bar `muted` and keeps red on the figure itself (a `TableCell tone="danger"`). A danger tone for a shortage (never an outflow) would be an additive change if the owner wants the board's red bar.
- Bars are a spacing step 4 (16 px) tall with 4 px corners; the boards draw 14 to 16 px with 3 px corners.
